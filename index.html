Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the AuraGlow AI app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Ceren Dokmener (collectively, the "Application"). Ceren Dokmener is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The total time spent on the Application
  • The mobile operating system you use

Camera and Image Processing

The Application requires access to your device’s camera and photo library solely to provide personal color analysis, aura evaluation, and daily outfit feedback features. The photos you capture or select are transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to our third-party AI processing partner (OpenAI) strictly to generate real-time visual and stylistic insights. Your photos are never used to train public AI models, nor are raw images stored permanently on our external servers or sold to third parties. Images are processed in transient memory to generate your analysis and are discarded immediately thereafter.

Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain explicit consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.
